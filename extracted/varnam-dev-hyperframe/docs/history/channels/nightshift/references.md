## Lenses (Round 0)

Ten lenses built from observation, zero borrowed vocabulary.

1. **In Media Res Entry** — all videos begin mid-action, specific timestamp, sensory grounding
2. **Dissonance Pivot** — the paradox line after the hook that creates forward pull
3. **Irony Architecture** — the full video is built on a reversal that only resolves at the end
4. **Scale Oscillation** — macro/micro breathing, ~60-90s max on macro
5. **Second-Person Lens Switch** — selective tool for visceral/experiential sequences
6. **Thematic Section Title** — chapter headers as mini-arguments
7. **Systemic Villain** — antagonist is always a system, never a person
8. **Moral Complexity Ending** — complexity as thesis, not hedge
9. **Legacy Bridge** — closing connects past pattern to ongoing present
10. **Grotesque Specificity Peak** — heatmap confirms: visceral consequence moments get replayed, not information moments

## Visual Analysis (Gemini, Round 0)

**Dual-mode system confirmed:**
- Cinematic Painterly (~55-60%) — storytelling scenes
- Informational Diagram (~30-35%) — explanation/data, sketch on white/parchment
- Mode switch trigger: narration function change ("what happened" → "how it worked")

**Cut rates measured:**
- Exposition: 8-12 cpm
- Action sequences: 18-25 cpm (can spike 4+ cuts in 4 seconds)
- Climactic reveals: 15-20 cpm

**Color:** Limited palettes (2-3 dominant hues per scene), always one saturated accent (orange/red). Switches by emotional beat, not scene boundary.

**Distinctive technique:** Abstract conceptual visualization — show what things mean, not what they look like. (Gravitational black hole for imperial power; painterly color swabs for river raid topography.)

**Key note:** Nightshift explicitly claims no AI content ("no AI slop" in Pirate video outro). Their visuals are hand-illustrated Kurzgesagt-style vector/painterly animation — resource-intensive, brand-specific. Our channel copies the *principles* (dual-mode logic, abstract conceptual visualization, color-as-emotion) not the illustration style.
