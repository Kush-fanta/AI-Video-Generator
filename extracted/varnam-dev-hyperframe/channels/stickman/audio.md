# Stickman - Audio

Sound supports the voice. It adds punctuation and breathing room without becoming a second performer.

## Voice

Energetic, conversational, slightly fast, and clear enough that the argument never gets lost inside the joke.

## Score

Use light quirky electronic cues, not epic cinema. The music should feel like explainer energy that becomes slightly weirder as the topic gets worse.

Long stretches with just voice and a few effects are healthy. Silence improves deadpan lines.

## SFX

SFX form a quiet language:

- soft pops for new elements
- light whooshes for arrows and quick transitions
- gentle thuds for big numbers
- occasional record scratch or crickets for one joke beat
- typewriter or marker energy for text construction

Keep density moderate and levels tucked low. They should be felt before they are noticed.

## Mix

Narration stays dominant.

- SFX stay low under speech
- music sits well below narration
- the funniest lines often land in near-silence
