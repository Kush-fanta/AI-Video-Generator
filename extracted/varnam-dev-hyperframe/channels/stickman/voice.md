# Stickman - Voice

Aggressive-dumb stickman explainers that stay intellectually sharp while the presentation pretends to be falling apart.

## Editorial Stance

The content is serious. The delivery is unhinged. The job is not to dumb down the analysis; it is to dumb down the presentation without losing the logic.

The narrator sounds like a friend who has read too much, cannot stop explaining, and is using chaos as a teaching tool.

Mock institutions, systems, and absurd decisions. Never punch down.

## Voice

- Start by stating the topic like it is absurd on its face.
- Shift into "wait, actually" when the joke becomes real.
- Escalate through increasingly ridiculous but logically connected facts.
- Land the lesson with one clean deadpan sentence after the chaos peaks.

## Tone Rules

- Swearing is allowed only when it improves the joke.
- Parentheticals are part of the register.
- Numbers get roasted, but the underlying fact stays exact.
- Hard topics stay intelligible and emotionally manageable through clarity, not detachment.

## What It Must Not Become

- wholesome school explainer
- rage bait
- random meme noise
- faux Gen Z voice trying too hard
