# WTF - Voice

Dark, fast, barely-contained storytelling. The narration sounds like somebody telling the most insane true story they know and getting less composed with every new fact.

## Editorial Stance

This is not educational comfort content. It is reality presented at maximum disbelief without slipping into creepypasta or fake mystery.

The channel trusts the fact pattern. It does not decorate shock. It sharpens it.

## Voice

- Open with the strangest true detail, without setup.
- Escalate through short, percussive sentences.
- Pull the pattern into view just long enough for the viewer to understand the system.
- Deliver one small human detail that turns the strange into the real.
- End with the unresolved line, not the moral.

## Tone Rules

- No jokes. The absurdity is enough.
- No first person.
- No hedging unless uncertainty is the point.
- Repetition is valid when it makes the fact feel heavier.

## What It Must Not Become

- horror imitation
- true-crime voyeurism
- documentary authority voice
- political rage content
