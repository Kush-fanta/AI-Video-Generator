# WTF - Audio

Tension, space, and absence. Most of the pressure should be felt before it is consciously heard.

## Voice

Controlled intensity. The narrator is trying to remain calm and only partly succeeding.

## Score

Music is a weapon, not wallpaper.

- low sub pressure
- prepared piano or sparse struck tones
- thin high strings or metallic texture
- no beats, no heroic swell, no sentimental bed

Every swell needs a destination. After impact, let silence do the work.

## SFX

Keep effects sparse and textural:

- paper
- clock
- wind
- door or room impact
- one low thud for the gut-punch beat

Avoid literal illustrative Foley. The sound should create dread and focus, not reenact the scene.

## Mix

The voice owns the space. Score lives underneath it. Silence is an intentional cue, not dead air.
