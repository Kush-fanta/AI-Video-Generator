# WTF - Visuals

Dark cinema with archival texture and brutal restraint. Things should emerge from black and sink back into it.

## Core Principle

The frame should feel like a blackout room with one spotlight. Use negative space as pressure, not emptiness.

## Visual World

- archival stills, paintings, artifacts, and documents first
- generated images only when no real visual evidence exists
- text cards that carry one idea at a time
- giant numbers or dates when the fact itself is the blow
- maps kept dark and minimal

## Composition

- full bleed, no decorative borders
- one visual idea per frame
- strong contrast, sparse labeling
- long holds when the beat needs discomfort

## Motion

- slow zooms into detail
- no slide-in energy
- text appears through opacity, not travel
- hard cuts inside fact pileups
- longer fades only between chapters

## Image Treatment

Real media should be desaturated, textured, and folded into the channel language. Generated images should stay painterly and period-aware, never glossy or AI-shiny.
