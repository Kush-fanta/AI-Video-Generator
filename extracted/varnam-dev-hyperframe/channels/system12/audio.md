# System12 — Audio

The score starts restrained and grows in authority. Not chest-thumping. Not devotional. Not action-trailer noise.

## Palette

Low pulse, warm synth bed, restrained percussion, deep tom accents, subtle Indian texture in select bridges, lifted brass or string bloom only at earned crest moments.

## Arc

- Opening: low-frequency ignition, measured, almost mechanical
- Mid-film: rhythm gains propulsion as the system widens and speeds up
- Digital beat: sharper percussive ticks, cleaner high-end, modern pulse
- Proof beat: broaden the harmonic bed, let the confidence breathe
- Ending: open and rising, not resolved into nostalgia; the track should imply runway ahead

## SFX

Use selectively: card tap, UI pulse, line-draw ticks, train rush, roadway hum, subtle power-on texture. Keep them elegant and mixed under the narration.

## Voice

Male Hindi/Hinglish voice with gravity and clarity. Commanding without sounding like a rally speech. He sounds like he knows the numbers and doesn't need to shout them.
