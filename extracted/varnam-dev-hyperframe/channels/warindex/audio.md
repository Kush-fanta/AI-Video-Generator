# WarIndex - Audio

WarIndex should sound like controlled escalation in a sealed room.

## Voice

Male narrator. Dry, close-mic, disciplined. The delivery should feel less like performance and more like a calm briefing delivered by someone who already knows the timeline cold.

Target pace: 125-145 WPM.

The key trait is restraint. The drama comes from the fact pattern, not from a theatrical read.

## Score Palette

Use a narrow palette:

- low sub pulse
- restrained synth drone
- metallic ticks and clockwork percussion
- distant toms or low taiko accents
- thin high strings for consequence
- brief piano or single-note motif for aftermath
- radio texture or filtered air as atmospheric glue

Forbidden:

- patriotic brass swells
- heroic choir
- trailer braaams
- busy hybrid-action percussion
- constant emotional piano bed

## Scoring Logic

The score follows operational phases:

1. **Hook:** low drone, faint metallic motion, room pressure
2. **Constraint stack:** ticks, sparse pulses, tightening rhythm
3. **Execution window:** percussion enters, tempo firms up
4. **Impact line:** music either slams or cuts out completely
5. **Aftermath:** strips down, often to one motif plus room tone

The best WarIndex moment is often a decisive sentence landing over almost nothing.

## Silence

Silence is not absence. It is the release valve after compression.

Use designed silence after:

- a refusal
- a capability mismatch
- a clean operational verdict
- a betrayal or reversal in the closing section

If the line is strong enough, let the room tone carry it.

## SFX

SFX are informational, not cinematic garnish.

- radar ping
- route trace click
- distant rotor or engine swell
- sealed-door impact
- map node pulse
- short radio burst
- restrained alarm texture for denial/failure

No whoosh-pack editing. No exaggerated weapon hits. No blockbuster Foley.

## Mix

Voice stays dominant. Music should feel under the floor, not on top of the words.

- VO always clear on laptop and phone speakers
- low end present but controlled
- percussion tucked below narration
- highs used sparingly so labels and hard cuts feel crisp

The mix should feel expensive because it never fights for attention.
