# Test Scenes

5 scenes covering the core scene types. Same spec for both tracks.

---

## Scene 1: Data Hero — "520 Million"

**Type:** Big number reveal
**Duration:** 6s (180 frames)
**Content:**
- Hero number: `520M`
- Label: "Bank accounts opened under Jan Dhan Yojana"
- Source: "Reserve Bank of India, 2024"
**Composition:** Left-anchored. Number at 120-140px serif, terracotta on landing. Label below in sans 36px. Accent line grows between number and label. Source bottom-left, muted caps. Right side: thin vertical terracotta accent line at 60% height.

---

## Scene 2: Comparison — "Old vs New"

**Type:** A vs B split
**Duration:** 8s (240 frames)
**Content:**
- Left: label "Traditional Banking", value "3-5 days", subtitle "NEFT/RTGS settlement"
- Right: label "UPI", value "Instant", subtitle "24/7, including holidays", accent color terracotta
- Headline: "Settlement speed"
- Source: "RBI Payment Systems Report"
**Composition:** Two columns, equal width, vertical divider line in P.light. Left column enters first (reveal at f=10), right column enters 20 frames later. Right column's value in terracotta to draw the eye. Headline above in sans caps, muted.

---

## Scene 3: Narrative Pivot — "The Shift"

**Type:** Before/after reframe
**Duration:** 6s (180 frames)
**Content:**
- Before: "India was a cash economy. 98% of transactions were physical."
- After: "Today, India leads the world in digital payments — by a factor of 5."
- Pivot happens at frame 70
**Composition:** "Before" text centered in muted serif italic, fades to 0.15 opacity at pivot. Hard visual shift — "After" text enters in regular serif, darker, slightly larger. A terracotta accent line grows below the after text. The pivot should feel like a page turn, not a crossfade.

---

## Scene 4: Step Sequence — "The Stack"

**Type:** Ordered process/layers
**Duration:** 10s (300 frames)
**Content:**
- Step 1: "Aadhaar" — "Biometric identity for 1.4 billion"
- Step 2: "Jan Dhan" — "Zero-balance bank accounts at scale"
- Step 3: "Mobile" — "Smartphones below ₹5,000"
- Step 4: "UPI" — "Open, interoperable payment rail"
- Category: "INDIA STACK"
- Source: "iSPIRT / NPCI"
**Composition:** Vertical layout. Each step has a numbered circle (terracotta, 56px) connected by a spine line that grows downward. Title in serif 42px, description in sans 28px below. Steps enter top-to-bottom with 25-frame stagger. Spring physics on circle scale.

---

## Scene 5: Climax — "The Thesis"

**Type:** Dark punch / thesis moment
**Duration:** 7s (210 frames)
**Content:**
- Setup (cream bg, muted italic): "When the infrastructure is free..."
- Punch (dark bg, terracotta): "everyone builds on it."
- Subtitle: "UPI processed 16.6 billion transactions in March 2025"
- Punch hits at frame 70
**Composition:** Two-phase. Phase 1: setup text fades in centered, italic serif 56px, muted color. At punch frame: HARD CUT to P.dark background. Punch text SLAMS in at 136px serif terracotta, scale from 1.14→1.0 with spring overshoot (damping 12, stiffness 90). Accent line grows below. Subtitle reveals 30 frames after punch, small sans muted.
